**Quantum entanglement** is a phenomenon where pairs or groups of particles become linked in such a way that the state of one particle instantaneously influences the state of another, no matter how far apart they are. This occurs because the entangled particles share a single quantum state, and measuring one particle directly affects the state of the other.

2. **Measurement and Collapse**: When a measurement is made on one of the entangled particles, the superposition collapses for both particles. This collapse affects the entangled partner instantaneously, regardless of the distance separating them, which is a hallmark of quantum entanglement.
