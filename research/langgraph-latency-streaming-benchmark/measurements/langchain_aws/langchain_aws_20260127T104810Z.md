**Antitrust laws** are regulations designed to promote competition and protect consumers by preventing monopolistic practices. These laws aim to ensure that businesses do not engage in practices that could stifle competition, leading to higher prices, reduced quality, and limited innovation.

#### Impact on Market Competition
Antitrust laws help maintain a competitive market environment by:
1. **Preventing Monopolies:** By breaking up or regulating monopolies, antitrust laws ensure that no single entity can dominate the market.
