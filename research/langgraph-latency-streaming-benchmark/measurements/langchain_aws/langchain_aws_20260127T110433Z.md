**Definition:**
Exothermic reactions are chemical reactions that release energy to their surroundings, usually in the form of heat. This results in an increase in the temperature of the surroundings.

### Endothermic Reactions

**Definition:**
Endothermic reactions are chemical reactions that absorb energy from their surroundings, usually in the form of heat. This results in a decrease in the temperature of the surroundings.

**Criteria:**
- **Energy Absorption:** The system gains energy.
- **Temperature Change:** The temperature of the surroundings decreases.
